# 🇨🇴 Colombian Flavors 🫓

Celebrate the heartwarming soul, biodiversity, and comforting home cooking of Colombia on Jorbites!

From March 2 to 22, we journey to the Andean peaks, Caribbean coasts, and coffee-growing valleys of Colombia. Known for its generous spirit and vibrant ingredients—native potatoes, sweet plantains, corn arepas, fresh cheeses, avocado, and fragrant hogao sauce—Colombian cuisine is pure comfort and flavor.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Colombian recipe or a dish inspired by authentic Colombian traditions during the event period will receive the exclusive Colombian Flavors badge.

### 🫓 Hearty ideas to inspire you:

- **Ajiaco Santafereño**: Iconic Bogotano soup made with three varieties of potatoes, tender shredded chicken, sweet corn on the cob, guascas herbs, capers, and cream.
- **Arepas de Choclo**: Sweet corn griddle cakes with melted golden butter and fresh farmer's cheese.
- **Bandeja Paisa**: Legendary feast plate with crispy chicharrón, red beans, ground beef, fried egg, plantain, rice, and avocado.
- **Pandebono & Buñuelos**: Warm, chewy cheese breads made with cassava starch and fresh curd cheese.
- **Sancocho**: Hearty slow-simmered family stew of beef, chicken, or fish with plantain, yucca, and corn.
- **Empanadas Colombianas**: Crispy yellow corn dough stuffed with spiced shredded beef and potatoes, served with spicy ají casero.

Warm up the griddle, stir your stews, and share the delicious joy of Colombian cooking! 🇨🇴✨
