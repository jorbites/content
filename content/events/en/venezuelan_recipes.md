# 🇻🇪 Venezuelan Flavors Festival 🫓

Join us for a celebration of Venezuela's most delicious dishes on Jorbites!

From January 5-31, share your favorite Venezuelan recipes. Whether you're making arepas, pabellón criollo, tequeños, or cachapas, this is your chance to explore the vibrant tastes of Venezuela.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Venezuelan recipe during the festival period will receive the exclusive Venezuelan Flavors badge.

### 🌽 Recipe ideas to inspire you:

- **Arepas**: The iconic cornmeal patties, perfect with any filling.
- **Pabellón Criollo**: The national dish, with shredded beef, black beans, rice, and plantains.
- **Tequeños**: Delicious cheese sticks wrapped in dough.
- **Cachapas**: Sweet corn pancakes, often served with cheese.
- **Hallacas**: A traditional Christmas dish, similar to tamales.
- **Sancocho**: A hearty and flavorful stew.
- **Patacones**: Fried plantain slices, a popular snack.
- **Quesillo**: A creamy and sweet caramel flan.

### 🌟 Why Venezuelan cuisine is special:

Venezuelan food offers a unique blend of indigenous, African, and European influences:
- Rich in flavor and history
- Features fresh, tropical ingredients
- Perfect for sharing with family and friends
- Versatile for any meal of the day
