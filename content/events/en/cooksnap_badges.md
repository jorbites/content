### 📸🍳 Cooksnap Badges in Jorbites! 🍳📸

Turn your kitchen creations into achievements! Whenever you prepare a recipe from Jorbites and share a photo of your dish using **"I Made This!" (Cooksnap)**, you earn chef XP and unlock exclusive achievement badges:

- 🥑 **Kitchen Explorer (Bronze)** - Cooked and shared 1 dish

![Kitchen Explorer](/images/events/cooksnap_badges/kitchen_explorer_1.webp)

- 🍳 **Cooksnap Veteran (Silver)** - Cooked and shared 5 dishes

![Cooksnap Veteran](/images/events/cooksnap_badges/cooksnap_master_5.webp)

- 👑 **Cooksnap Master (Gold)** - Cooked and shared 15 dishes

![Cooksnap Master](/images/events/cooksnap_badges/cooksnap_master_15.webp)

Cook, snap, share, and inspire the Jorbites community! 🚀✨
