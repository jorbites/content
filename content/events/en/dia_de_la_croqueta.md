# 🥔 Croquette Day 🥔

Join us in celebrating International Croquette Day on Jorbites!

**January 15th** is International Croquette Day, honoring one of the most beloved and comforting dishes in Spanish cuisine. Croquettes are crispy on the outside, creamy on the inside, and absolutely irresistible!

### 🏆 Win your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a recipe featuring croquettes as a main dish **between January 15th and 18th** will be rewarded with the exclusive Croquette Day badge.

We're giving you extra time to perfect your recipe and share your croquette creations with the community!

### 🥔 Ideas to inspire you:

- **Classic ham croquettes**: The traditional favorite that never fails
- **Creamy mushroom croquettes**: Earthy flavors in every bite
- **Seafood croquettes**: Cod, shrimp, or mixed seafood delights
- **Cheese croquettes**: Melty, gooey perfection
- **Innovative vegan croquettes**: Spinach, pumpkin, or sweet potato
- **Fusion croquettes**: Mix cultures and create unique combinations

### 💛 Why do we celebrate croquettes?

Croquettes are more than just a tapa; they are a culinary tradition that:
- Brings families together in the kitchen
- Transforms leftovers into delicious creations
- Offers endless flavor possibilities
- Represents comfort food at its finest

Celebrate with us and share your croquette masterpieces! 🥔✨
