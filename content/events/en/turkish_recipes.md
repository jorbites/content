# 🇹🇷 Turkish Flavors 🫓

Journey through the historic crossroads of flavor with the vibrant cuisine of Turkey on Jorbites!

From February 9 to March 1, we celebrate Turkish gastronomy—a magnificent culinary bridge connecting the Mediterranean, the Balkans, and the Middle East. Renowned for its rich mezze culture, charcoal-grilled skewers, boat-shaped pide flatbreads, comforting soups, and syrup-drenched pastries, Turkish food is a feast of texture and aroma.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Turkish recipe or a dish inspired by traditional Turkish ingredients during the event period will earn the exclusive Turkish Flavors badge.

### 🫓 Bountiful ideas to inspire you:

- **Turkish Pide**: Boat-shaped baked flatbread topped with spiced minced meat (kıyma), melting cheese (kaşar), and baked egg.
- **Mercimek Çorbası**: Velvety, warming red lentil soup seasoned with cumin, dried mint, and sizzling paprika-butter drizzle.
- **Köfte & Sis Kebab**: Spiced grilled meatballs or tender skewers served with sumac onion salad and flatbread.
- **Menemen**: Soft-scrambled eggs simmered with sweet peppers, ripe tomatoes, olive oil, and herbs.
- **Börek**: Flaky golden filo pastry rolls layered with crumbled feta cheese, spinach, or potato filling.
- **Baklava & Turkish Delight (Lokum)**: Crisp layers of phyllo stuffed with chopped pistachios, baked golden and soaked in fragrant sugar syrup.

Bake your flatbreads, melt your spiced butter, and share the vibrant hospitality of Turkey! 🇹🇷✨
