# 🎄 Christmas Contest 🎄

Our annual Christmas contest is back! All Jorbiters are invited to share their most festive recipes for a chance to win an exclusive badge. The winner will be decided by the community through a series of votes on Instagram.

## 🧁 How to Participate

Share your best holiday recipe between December 20th and December 29th, 2026. Whether it's a traditional family dish or a modern festive creation, we want to see what you're cooking up for the holidays!

## 🗳️ Voting

The voting will take place in different phases on our Instagram account on December 30th. Stay tuned to our stories and posts to support your favorite recipes!

## 🏆 Prize

The creator of the winning recipe will receive an exclusive Christmas winner badge on their Jorbites profile. The winner will be announced in this event post after the community vote.

Good luck to all participants, and may the best recipe win! Merry cooking and happy holidays! 🎅🎁
