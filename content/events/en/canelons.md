# 🥘 Canelons 🥘

Celebrate one of the greatest culinary traditions of the holiday season on Jorbites: bubbling, oven-baked cannelloni!

Rooted in the beloved Sant Esteve holiday (December 26), traditional canelons were created as the ultimate festive zero-waste dish: taking the delicious slow-roasted meats of Christmas dinner and wrapping them into tender pasta rolls smothered in creamy béchamel and golden grated cheese.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a cannelloni recipe between December 25 and December 30 will receive the exclusive Canelons badge.

### 🥘 Delicious ideas to inspire you:

- **Traditional Sant Esteve Roast Canelons**: Slow-cooked beef, pork, and chicken with rustic pâté and nutmeg béchamel.
- **Spinach, Ricotta & Pine Nut Canelons**: Vegetarian perfection with a hint of garlic and cream.
- **Wild Mushroom & Truffle Canelons**: Earthy seasonal boletus with silky white sauce.
- **Seafood Canelons**: Monkfish, prawns, and crab draped in rich shellfish bisque sauce.
- **Duck Confit & Caramelised Onion Canelons**: Sweet, savory, and unmistakably festive.
- **Crispy Gratin Crust**: Golden Manchego or Gruyère bubbling straight out of the oven.
