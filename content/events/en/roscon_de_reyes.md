# 👑 King's Cake 👑

Ring in the Epiphany with the royal tradition of homemade *Roscón de Reyes* and *Tortell de Reis* on Jorbites!

From January 1 to 7, celebrate the festive brioche crowned with candied jewels. Whether you knead a traditional orange blossom brioche filled with sweet whipped cream, rich truffle, marzipan, or classic pastry cream, don't forget to hide the lucky king figurine and the dry bean!

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Roscón, Tortell, Galette des Rois, or Epiphany brioche recipe during the event period will earn the exclusive King's Cake badge.

### 👑 Royal ideas to inspire you:

- **Traditional Orange Blossom Brioche**: Scented with orange zest, lemon, and real *agua de azahar*.
- **Whipped Cream & Truffle Roscón**: Sliced and filled with decadent dairy cream or chocolate.
- **Classic Catalan Tortell de Reis**: Enriched with marzipan filling and candied cherries.
- **French Galette des Rois**: Puff pastry filled with rich almond frangipane.
- **Vegan Roscón**: Light, fluffy plant-based brioche with olive oil and citrus.
- **Crown Toppings**: Candied melon, oranges, cherries, and crunchy sugar crystals.

Knead the dough, hide the surprise, and crown your table like a king! 👑✨
