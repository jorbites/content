# 🇻🇳 Vietnamese Flavors 🍜

Immerse yourself in the delicate lightness, freshness, and street-food mastery of Vietnam on Jorbites!

From January 19 to February 8, we celebrate Vietnamese gastronomy—celebrated for its clean broths, abundant bundles of fresh herbs (mint, cilantro, Thai basil), and world-famous balance of texture. From slow-simmered bone broths to crusty baguettes with savory fillings, Vietnamese cuisine is both refreshing and deeply comforting.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Vietnamese recipe or a dish inspired by Vietnamese culinary heritage during the event period will earn the exclusive Vietnamese Flavors badge.

### 🍜 Street-food ideas to inspire you:

- **Phở Bò or Phở Gà**: Steaming, crystal-clear beef or chicken noodle soup infused with star anise, cinnamon, charred ginger, and fresh lime.
- **Bánh Mì**: Crusty baguette loaded with savory grilled pork or tofu, pâté, pickled daikon and carrot, cucumber, and fresh cilantro.
- **Gỏi Cuốn (Fresh Summer Rolls)**: Translucent rice paper rolls filled with prawns, herbs, and vermicelli noodles with hoisin-peanut dip.
- **Bún Chả**: Grilled savory pork patties served over thin rice vermicelli noodles, fresh herbs, and dipping nuoc cham sauce.
- **Bánh Xèo**: Crispy, turmeric-golden savory pancakes stuffed with shrimp, bean sprouts, and wrapped in leafy greens.
- **Cà Phê Trứng (Vietnamese Egg Coffee)**: Rich dark drip coffee crowned with a luscious whipped egg yolk and condensed milk cream.

Simmer your broth, roll your rice paper, and share your best Vietnamese dishes! 🇻🇳✨
