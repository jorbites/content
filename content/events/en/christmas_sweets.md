# 🍪 Christmas Sweets 🍪

Fill your kitchen with holiday cheer and the aroma of baking spices on Jorbites!

From December 1 to 18, we celebrate the magic of holiday confections and festive baking. Whether you are whipping up homemade turrón, cutting out spiced gingerbread cookies, rolling delicate polvorones, or rolling a chocolate Yule log (*Bûche de Noël* / *Tronco de Navidad*), this is your time to share sweet holiday joy.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a festive holiday dessert or sweet recipe during the event period will earn the exclusive Christmas Sweets badge.

### 🍪 Sweet ideas to inspire you:

- **Artisanal Turrón**: Classic soft Jijona, crunchy Alicante, chocolate-praline, or toasted yolk.
- **Gingerbread Cookies**: Decorated with royal icing for the Christmas tree or cookie tins.
- **Melt-in-your-mouth Polvorones**: Traditional almond and cinnamon shortbreads.
- **Neules & Wafers**: Delicate rolled wafers paired with cava or dipped in chocolate.
- **Yule Log (Tronco de Navidad)**: Rolled sponge cake filled with cream or chocolate ganache.
- **Spiced Panforte or Stollen**: Packed with dried fruits, nuts, and winter spices.

Preheat the oven, dust the counter with powdered sugar, and share your sweetest festive treats! 🍪✨
