# 🦃 Thanksgiving Feast 🥧

Join us for a celebration of gratitude and delicious food on Jorbites!

From November 25-30, share your favorite Thanksgiving recipes. Whether you're roasting a turkey, baking a classic pumpkin pie, or preparing your favorite side dishes, this is your chance to share the warmth and flavors of the holiday.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Thanksgiving recipe during the festival period will receive the exclusive Thanksgiving Feast badge.

### 🍁 Recipe ideas to inspire you:

- **Roast Turkey**: The centerpiece of any Thanksgiving meal.
- **Pumpkin Pie**: A classic holiday dessert.
- **Cranberry Sauce**: Sweet, tart, and easy to make.
- **Sweet Potato Casserole**: A comforting and delicious side dish.
- **Green Bean Casserole**: A Thanksgiving staple.
- **Mashed Potatoes**: Creamy, buttery, and irresistible.
- **Stuffing**: A savory and aromatic classic.
- **Pecan Pie**: A sweet and nutty treat.

### 🌟 Why Thanksgiving dishes are special:

Thanksgiving recipes are all about comfort, tradition, and sharing:
- Rich in flavor and history
- Perfect for family gatherings
- A celebration of the harvest
- Versatile for leftovers
- A chance to create new traditions

### 🎯 Challenge yourself:

Put your own spin on a classic Thanksgiving dish or share a family recipe that has been passed down through generations. Let's make this a Thanksgiving to remember! 🦃✨
