# 🇹🇭 Thai Flavors 🍜

Awaken your senses with the vibrant balance and tropical freshness of Thailand on Jorbites!

From December 29 to January 18, we celebrate Thai cuisine—an artful harmony of sweet, sour, salty, bitter, and fiery spicy flavors. Featuring fragrant lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, galangal, fresh chili, coconut milk, and aromatic Thai basil, each bite is a burst of energy and delicious complexity.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Thai recipe or a dish celebrating traditional Thai ingredients during the event period will earn the exclusive Thai Flavors badge.

### 🍜 Bright ideas to inspire you:

- **Pad Thai**: Iconic stir-fried rice noodles tossed with tamarind sauce, tofu or prawns, crunchy bean sprouts, and crushed peanuts.
- **Thai Green or Red Curry (Gaeng Kiew Wan)**: Creamy coconut curry with bamboo shoots, chicken or vegetables, and fresh Thai basil.
- **Tom Yum Goong**: Hot and sour aromatic prawn soup infused with lemongrass, galangal, lime juice, and chili.
- **Tom Kha Gai**: Silky, comforting chicken soup simmered in coconut milk with fragrant galangal and mushrooms.
- **Som Tum (Papaya Salad)**: Crisp green papaya pounded with garlic, lime, peanuts, and bird's eye chilies.
- **Mango Sticky Rice (Khao Niew Mamuang)**: Warm, sweet coconut-infused sticky rice served with ripe golden mango slices.

Fire up your wok, crush your aromatics in the mortar, and share your best Thai creations! 🇹🇭✨
