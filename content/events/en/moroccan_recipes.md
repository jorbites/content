# 🇲🇦 Moroccan Flavors 🍲

Embark on an aromatic culinary journey through the vibrant flavors of Morocco on Jorbites!

From November 17 to December 7, we celebrate one of the world's most fragrant and comforting culinary traditions. From slow-simmered earthen tagines to fluffy steamed couscous, Moroccan cooking masterfully blends savory, sweet, and spiced notes using ras el hanout, preserved lemons, saffron, ginger, and fresh mint.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Moroccan recipe or a dish inspired by authentic Moroccan ingredients during the event period will earn the exclusive Moroccan Flavors badge.

### 🍲 Recipe ideas to inspire you:

- **Slow-Cooked Tagine**: Tender lamb with prunes and toasted almonds, or chicken with preserved lemons and green olives.
- **Traditional Couscous**: Steamed over fragrant broth with seven seasonal vegetables and chickpeas.
- **Harira Soup**: Rich, comforting tomato, lentil, and chickpea soup perfumed with coriander and ginger.
- **Zaalouk & Briouats**: Smoky roasted eggplant and tomato dip, or crispy spiced meat pastries wrapped in warqa.
- **Pastilla (Bastilla)**: Crisp, flaky pastry layered with spiced poultry, cinnamon, sugar, and crunchy almonds.
- **Mint Tea & Sweet Treats**: Moroccan mint tea paired with gazelle horns (*cornes de gazelle*) or almond chebakia.

Dust off your spices, simmer your clay pots, and share the warmth of Moroccan hospitality! 🇲🇦✨
