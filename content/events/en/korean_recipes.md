# 🇰🇷 Korean Flavors 🥢

Experience the irresistible crunch, savory depth, and fermented magic of Korean cuisine on Jorbites!

From December 8 to 28, we explore the dynamic culinary landscape of South Korea. Built upon balance, fermentation, and bold seasonings like gochujang, gochugaru, toasted sesame oil, and garlic, Korean food offers the ultimate combination of warmth and vibrant flavors.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a Korean recipe or a dish inspired by Korean culinary traditions during the event period will receive the exclusive Korean Flavors badge.

### 🥢 Delicious ideas to inspire you:

- **Bibimbap Bowl**: Rice topped with sautéed seasoned vegetables, marinated beef or tofu, fried egg, and gochujang.
- **Kimchi-jjigae**: Steaming, spicy winter stew packed with well-fermented kimchi, pork belly, and silky tofu.
- **Crispy Korean Fried Chicken (KFC)**: Double-fried ultra-crispy wings tossed in sweet-and-spicy gochujang or soy-garlic glaze.
- **Tteokbokki**: Chewy rice cakes simmered in a sweet, fiery red chili sauce with fish cakes and scallions.
- **Japchae**: Stir-fried sweet potato glass noodles with crisp colorful veggies, mushrooms, and sesame.
- **Pajeon (Scallion Pancakes)**: Crispy, savory pan-fried pancakes loaded with scallions and seafood, served with soy dipping sauce.

Grab your chopsticks, bring out the kimchi, and share your best K-Food creations! 🇰🇷✨
