# 🥣 Bowls & Broths 🥗

Reset, recharge, and warm up your January with wholesome goodness on Jorbites!

From January 8 to 22, we spotlight the comforting simplicity of nourishing grain bowls and deep, steaming broths. After the rich holiday feasts, nothing feels better than a steaming bowl of ginger-infused bone broth, soothing miso soup, ramen, or vibrant grain bowls layered with roasted winter veggies and gut-friendly ferments.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a nourishing warm bowl or homemade broth recipe during the event period will earn the exclusive Bowls & Broths badge.

### 🥣 Nourishing ideas to inspire you:

- **Slow-Simmered Bone or Vegetable Broth**: Golden broth steeped with ginger, turmeric, garlic, and winter herbs.
- **Warming Ramen & Miso Bowls**: Rich dashi or mushroom broth with chewy noodles, tofu, and greens.
- **Winter Harvest Grain Bowl**: Quinoa, farro, or wild rice topped with roasted squash, kale, and tahini drizzle.
- **Hearty Lentil & Turmeric Bowl**: Earthy legumes paired with pickled red onion and fresh herbs.
- **Clear Broth with Dumplings**: Delicate wontons or matzo balls floating in fragrant steaming broth.
- **Sesame & Tofu Nourish Bowl**: Crisp pan-seared tofu, edamame, avocado, and pickled ginger.

Grab your favorite wide bowl, simmer up warmth, and share your feel-good winter recipes! 🥣✨
