### 🔍🌟 Most Searched Badges in Jorbites! 🌟🔍

Turn community curiosity into culinary achievements! In Jorbites, recipes that trend, spark excitement, and capture everyone's appetite by becoming the most searched dishes across the platform earn exclusive distinction badges:

- 🌟 **Most Searched of the Month** - Awarded to the recipe with the highest number of searches during the month!

![Most Searched of the Month](/images/events/most_searched/most_searched_month.webp)

- 👑 **Most Searched of the Year** - The ultimate culinary hallmark: awarded to the most searched recipe across the entire community for the year!

![Most Searched of the Year](/images/events/most_searched/most_searched_year.webp)

Share your best recipes, inspire fellow foodies, and see your dishes climb to the top! 🚀🥑
