# 🌰 Castanyada 🌰

Celebrate the cozy flavors of deep autumn on Jorbites!

From November 10 to 22, we honor the beloved tradition of roasted chestnuts, sweet potatoes (*moniatos* / *boniatos*), and traditional autumn sweets like *panellets*. As the temperatures drop, nothing brings more warmth than the aroma of street-roasted chestnuts and oven-baked fall comfort.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge!

All Jorbiters who publish a recipe featuring chestnuts, sweet potatoes, pumpkin, or traditional autumn festival sweets during the event period will earn the exclusive Castanyada badge.

### 🌰 Recipe ideas to inspire you:

- **Roasted chestnuts**: Traditional pan-roasted with a crisp slit or baked in the oven.
- **Chestnut & mushroom soup**: Velvety, earthy, and perfectly warming.
- **Baked sweet potato with cinnamon**: Simple comfort, caramelised in its own skin.
- **Traditional panellets**: Almond marzipan coated in pine nuts, cocoa, or coconut.
- **Sweet potato and chestnut gnocchi**: A fall twist on an Italian staple.
- **Chestnut cream & chocolate tart**: Rich, decadent, and autumnal.

Light up the oven, warm up your kitchen, and share your best autumn creations! 🌰✨
