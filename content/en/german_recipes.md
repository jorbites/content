# 🇩🇪 German Recipes 🍽️

Explore the rich culinary traditions of Germany on Jorbites!

Share recipes inspired by German cuisine—from comforting classics to regional specialties. Whether it's a savory main course, a traditional side dish, or a famous dessert, we want to see your take on German flavors.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge

All Jorbiters who publish a German-inspired recipe during the event window will receive the German Recipes badge.

### 🍽️ Ideas to inspire you

- **Sauerbraten**: Traditional pot roast.
- **Pretzels (Brezeln)**: Iconic knot-shaped bread.
- **Schnitzel**: Thin, breaded and fried meat (or plant-based alternatives).
- **Käsespätzle**: German-style mac and cheese.
- **Kartoffelsalat**: Classic German potato salad.
- **Bratwurst**: Grilled German sausages.
- **Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte**: Black Forest Cake.
- **Apfelstrudel**: Traditional apple strudel.

### 🌿 What makes it special

German cuisine is known for its heartiness, regional diversity, and focus on high-quality ingredients. From the Alpine traditions in the South to the maritime flavors of the North, it offers a wide range of comforting and delicious dishes perfect for any season. 🇩🇪
