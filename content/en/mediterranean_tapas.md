# 🧆 Mediterranean Tapas Festival 🍷

Experience the joy of sharing small plates on Jorbites!

From September 15 to 28, share your favorite tapas, appetizers, and mezze platters inspired by Mediterranean coastal flavors.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge
Publish a tapas or sharing bite recipe during the festival window to earn the Mediterranean Tapas badge.

### 💡 Ideas to inspire you
- **Patatas Bravas & Croquettes**: Classic crispy bites with savory sauces.
- **Marinated Olives & Manchego**: Simple, high-quality appetizer spreads.
- **Garlic Shrimp / Mushrooms**: Quick, fragrant tapas.
- **Pan con Tomate**: Simple perfection with olive oil and ripe tomatoes.
