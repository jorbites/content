# 🇬🇷 Greek Recipes 🍽️

Explore the rich culinary traditions of Greece on Jorbites!

Share recipes inspired by Greek cuisine—from fresh Mediterranean classics to regional specialties. Whether it's a savory main course, a traditional side dish, or a famous dessert, we want to see your take on Greek flavors.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge

All Jorbiters who publish a Greek-inspired recipe during the event window will receive the Greek Recipes badge.

### 🍽️ Ideas to inspire you

- **Moussaka**: Layered eggplant, potato, and spiced minced meat.
- **Souvlaki**: Grilled skewers of seasoned meat.
- **Tzatziki**: Creamy yogurt, cucumber, and garlic dip.
- **Greek Salad (Horiatiki)**: Fresh tomatoes, cucumbers, olives, and feta cheese.
- **Spanakopita**: Flaky phyllo pastry filled with spinach and feta.
- **Dolmades**: Grape leaves stuffed with rice and herbs.
- **Baklava**: Rich, sweet dessert made of layers of phyllo and nuts.
- **Gyros**: Thinly sliced seasoned meat served in pita bread.

### 🌿 What makes it special

Greek cuisine is the heart of the Mediterranean diet, celebrated for its simplicity, freshness, and healthy ingredients. With its heavy use of olive oil, fresh vegetables, and aromatic herbs, it offers a delightful balance of flavors that have been perfected over thousands of years. 🇬🇷
