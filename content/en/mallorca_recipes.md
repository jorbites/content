# 🏝️ Mallorca Recipes 🍽️

Explore the rich culinary traditions of Mallorca on Jorbites!

Share recipes inspired by Mallorcan cuisine—from traditional pastries to savory Mediterranean dishes. Whether it's a classic main course, a unique snack, or a famous dessert, we want to see your take on the flavors of the island.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge

All Jorbiters who publish a Mallorca-inspired recipe during the event window will receive the Mallorca Recipes badge.

### 🍽️ Ideas to inspire you

- **Ensaimada**: Iconic spiral-shaped sweet pastry.
- **Sobrassada**: Traditional cured pork sausage with paprika.
- **Coca de Trampó**: Traditional vegetable flatbread.
- **Arròs Brut**: Hearty and spiced "dirty rice" with meat and vegetables.
- **Tumbet**: Classic baked vegetable layers.
- **Frit Mallorquí**: Traditional stir-fry of meat, potatoes, and peppers.
- **Panades**: Savory pies typically filled with meat or peas.
- **Crespells**: Traditional sweet biscuits.

### 🌿 What makes it special

Mallorcan cuisine is a jewel of the Mediterranean, characterized by its use of local ingredients like olive oil, fresh vegetables, and traditional pork products. From the coast to the Sierra de Tramuntana, the island offers a unique blend of flavors that reflect its rich history and culture. 🏝️
