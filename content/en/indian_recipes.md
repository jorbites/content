# 🇮🇳 Indian Recipes 🍽️

Celebrate the flavors of India on Jorbites!

Share recipes inspired by Indian cuisine—rich in spices, colors, and diverse traditions. From fragrant curries to street food classics and delicious desserts.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge

All Jorbiters who publish an Indian-inspired recipe during the event window will receive the Indian Recipes badge.

### 🍽️ Ideas to inspire you

- Butter Chicken (or plant-based version)
- Chana Masala (chickpea curry)
- Palak Paneer (spinach and cheese)
- Vegetable Biryani
- Samosas
- Garlic Naan
- Gulab Jamun (sweet milk dumplings)

### 🌿 What makes it special

Indian cuisine is a celebration of spices, aromatics, and regional diversity. It’s known for its bold flavors, vibrant colors, and incredible variety of vegetarian and vegan options. Bring your take and enjoy! 🇮🇳
